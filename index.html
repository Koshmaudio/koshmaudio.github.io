About me

Designing the sound of being there
I'm a sound designer and professional musician with a career spent playing everything from jazz and big bands to rock, production shows and symphony. In 2019, I got pulled into game audio by an idea I couldn't leave alone: sound doesn't have to play back the same way every time. It can react to a player, reveal a space, give an action weight or tell part of a story. I went headfirst into Unity, FMOD, Wwise and interactive audio, and I've been exploring that territory ever since. Today, I combine a musician's ear with digital synthesis, field recording and interactive audio implementation to create distinctive sound textures and responsive systems. I'm interested in both sides of the craft: making sounds worth listening to and building the logic that makes them feel like a natural part of a living world. I want sound to do more than accompany the experience. I want it to help create it.

A little more about me
Some of my field-recording work has taken me far beyond a typical sound hunt. One of my most unusual and challenging projects involved recording explosions and other sounds of the war in my hometown of Kharkiv. It was a very different kind of recording experience, and one I won't forget. Outside audio, I'm happiest exploring somewhere new, reading, getting lost in a good fantasy or medieval setting, or figuring out how something works. I love survival sandboxes, strategy games and RPGs, from The Long Dark and Project Zomboid to Frostpunk, Crusader Kings, Baldur's Gate, KOTOR, The Witcher 3 and Red Dead Redemption 2. I also like making and fixing things with my hands. Woodworking, metalworking, soldering, building furniture, making custom contact microphones and repairing electronics are all fair game. Give me an interesting problem and a chance to experiment, and I'll probably get stuck into it.

What I'm exploring
I'm particularly interested in the overlap between creative sound design and technical audio development. My current goals include developing hybrid synthesis and procedural-audio workflows, building efficient and reusable interactive audio systems, exploring new ways to tell stories through sound, and going deeper into game-engine integration. I'm also working toward more extensive DAW automation and scripting, alongside a bespoke sound library designed to be useful across different projects. The common thread is curiosity: finding better ways to create sounds, smarter ways to make them work in a game, and new ways for audio to shape what the player experiences.

Sound Design Showcase:

Nemorise short

During this short film, I served as both sound designer and composer, crafting a suspenseful audio world. Tackling sci-fi sound design and composition for the first time pushed me to explore new synthesis techniques and recording methods. To overcome the learning curve, I experimented with modular synthesis and granular processing, creating unique textures that felt both alien and organic. The result is a cohesive soundscape that supports the film’s eerie tension and narrative arcs.
My Role:
- Collaborated closely with the director to align audio cues with visual storytelling
- Designed bespoke sound effects that evoke an otherworldly, futuristic environment
- Composed an original score blending electronic elements with orchestral touches
- Mixed and mastered all audio elements to ensure clarity and dynamic contrast
